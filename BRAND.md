# Electorate — Brand & Copy Guidelines

## Platform
- **Name:** Electorate
- **Domain:** electorate.ng
- **Concept:** A Glassdoor-style platform where Nigerian constituents score their elected officials

---

## Core Branded Term: Electorating

The scoring system is called **Electorating** (capital E, always).

### Usage
- **Noun:** "Their Electorating stands at 3.2"
- **Verb:** "Start Electorating your officials"
- **Plural:** "Electoratings" (a collection of ratings)
- **Call to action:** "Rate them. Hold them accountable."

### Rules
- Never use generic terms like "community score", "community rating", or "review" where "Electorating" can replace them
- "Electorating" replaces both the act of rating and the score itself
- "Electoratings" is the plural for a collection

---

## Taglines

Use the most contextually appropriate:

| Context | Tagline |
|---------|---------|
| Hero / primary | "Rate them. Hold them accountable." |
| Secondary / emotive | "Your vote put them there. Your rating keeps them honest." |

---

## Tone

- **Citizen-first, credible, and direct**
- Authoritative enough for media and NGO citations
- Accessible enough for everyday Nigerians
- Never condescending; never partisan
